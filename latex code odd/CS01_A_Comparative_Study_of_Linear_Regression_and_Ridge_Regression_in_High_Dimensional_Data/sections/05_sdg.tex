\subsection{SDG 4: Quality Education}
\noindent\textbf{Target 4.3:} Ensure equal access for all women and men to affordable and quality technical, vocational and tertiary education. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 4.4:} Substantially increase the number of youth and adults who have relevant skills, including technical and vocational skills, for employment and decent jobs.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Enables predictive modeling of student academic progression, identifying at-risk learners early for targeted counseling.
  \item Supports evidence-based pedagogical adjustments and institutional interventions based on objective analytics.
  \item Equips students and future engineers with foundational competency in data-driven methodologies and statistical rigor.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} provides reproducible pedagogical case studies and predictive educational performance tracking frameworks.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Target 8.5:} Achieve full and productive employment and decent work for all women and men.

\end{itemize}
