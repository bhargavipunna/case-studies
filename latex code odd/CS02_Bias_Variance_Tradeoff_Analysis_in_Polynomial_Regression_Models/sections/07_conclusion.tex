\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}

\subsection{Summary of Contributions}
This case study provided a comprehensive theoretical and empirical investigation of \textbf{Bias--Variance Tradeoff Analysis in Polynomial Regression Models}. The major findings and engineering contributions include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Formulated the exact mathematical relationships governing parameter estimation, stability bounds, and regularization shrinkage.
  \item Demonstrated through empirical benchmarking that the proposed methodology reliably curbs overfitting, enhances numerical conditioning, and optimizes generalization.
  \item Provided actionable architectural guidelines for preventing data leakage in cross-validation pipelines and selecting cost-sensitive decision thresholds.
  \item Mapped the engineering solutions directly to institutional program outcomes and UN Sustainable Development Goals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Engineering Extensions}
Future developments can extend this work in several high-impact directions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Elastic Net and Sparsity Integration:} Combining $L_1$ and $L_2$ penalties to perform simultaneous variable selection and collinear grouping in ultra-high dimensions ($p > 10^5$).
  \item \textbf{Online and Streaming Adaptation:} Developing recursive, stochastic gradient estimators capable of updating parameter vectors in real-time under non-stationary streaming telemetry.
  \item \textbf{Federated Edge Execution:} Distributing the optimization across decentralized, privacy-preserving client nodes using differential privacy constraints.
\end{enumerate}
