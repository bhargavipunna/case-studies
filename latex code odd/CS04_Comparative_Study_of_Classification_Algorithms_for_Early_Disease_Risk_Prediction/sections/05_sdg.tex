\section{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Related to Comparative Study of Classification Algorithms for Early Disease Risk Prediction}
Engineering implementations must align with broader global sustainability imperatives. By modernizing legacy operations, eliminating resource inefficiencies, and ensuring transparent institutional governance, this case study directly supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\subsection{SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being}
\noindent\textbf{Target 3.8:} Achieve universal health coverage, including financial risk protection, access to quality essential health-care services and safe, effective, quality pharmaceuticals. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 3.d:} Strengthen the capacity of all countries, in particular developing countries, for early warning, risk reduction and management of national and global health risks.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Facilitates rapid disease risk prediction and diagnostic triage via non-invasive biomedical sensors and machine learning.
  \item Enables continuous real-time physiological telemetry, alerting clinical specialists instantly during acute anomalies.
  \item Democratizes preventive care and early diagnostic screening for underserved and remote patient populations.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} enables highly reliable, early clinical risk stratification and diagnostic triage, minimizing diagnostic errors in healthcare.
\end{itemize}
