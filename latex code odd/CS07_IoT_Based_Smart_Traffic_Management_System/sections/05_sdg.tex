\section{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Related to IoT-Based Smart Traffic Management System}
Engineering implementations must align with broader global sustainability imperatives. By modernizing legacy operations, eliminating resource inefficiencies, and ensuring transparent institutional governance, this case study directly supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\subsection{SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure}
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.1:} Develop quality, reliable, sustainable and resilient infrastructure to support economic development and human well-being. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.4:} Upgrade infrastructure and retrofit industries to make them sustainable, with increased resource-use efficiency. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.5:} Enhance scientific research, upgrade the technological capabilities of industrial sectors, and encourage innovation.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Deploys resilient edge sensors, distributed telemetry backbones, and robust network routing architectures.
  \item Modernizes industrial plant monitoring with predictive maintenance, preventing catastrophic equipment downtime.
  \item Fosters high-performance technological innovation through reproducible, benchmarked algorithmic implementations.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} modernizes physical industrial infrastructure with resilient wireless telemetry and predictive asset maintenance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities}
\noindent\textbf{Target 11.2:} Provide access to safe, affordable, accessible and sustainable transport systems for all, expanding public transport. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 11.6:} Reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities, including by paying special attention to air quality and municipal waste management.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Controls urban traffic signal timings dynamically to eliminate gridlock, reducing idling vehicular exhaust emissions.
  \item Establishes ubiquitous urban air quality sensor meshes, providing transparent pollution tracking and public awareness.
  \item Enhances metropolitan safety and surveillance efficiency through real-time edge computer vision and object detection.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} optimizes municipal traffic flow and establishes ubiquitous air quality sensing meshes to improve urban livability.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{SDG 13: Climate Action}
\noindent\textbf{Target 13.1:} Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards and natural disasters. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 13.3:} Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation and impact reduction.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Monitors localized greenhouse gas emissions and particulate concentrations to enforce carbon abatement policies.
  \item Supports adaptive agricultural irrigation and soil conservation in the face of shifting seasonal rainfall patterns.
  \item Improves electrical grid energy efficiency, directly lowering fossil fuel consumption at power generating stations.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} reduces greenhouse gas emissions from idling vehicles and power plants through intelligent telemetry-driven scheduling.
\end{itemize}
