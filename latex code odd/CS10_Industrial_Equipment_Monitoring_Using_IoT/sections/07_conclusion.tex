\section{Conclusion and Future Scope}

\subsection{Summary of Contributions}
This investigation successfully conceptualized, built, and evaluated \textbf{Industrial Equipment Monitoring Using IoT}. Key engineering contributions include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Engineered a low-power, end-to-end IoT architecture capable of multi-year unattended field operation.
  \item Derived mathematical formulations for sensor transduction, radio propagation, and energy harvesting profiles.
  \item Demonstrated empirical performance gains, including dramatic reductions in operational delay, energy usage, and material wastage.
  \item Aligned system outcomes directly with ABET criteria and UN Sustainable Development Goals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Engineering Horizons}
Emerging avenues for future development comprise:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{TinyML on the Edge:} Deploying quantized deep neural networks directly onto microcontrollers for on-device anomaly classification without cloud dependency.
  \item \textbf{Satellite IoT Integration:} Augmenting terrestrial networks with direct-to-satellite LoRaWAN (LEO satellite constellations) for ubiquitous coverage across oceanic and desert zones.
  \item \textbf{Decentralized Edge Consensus:} Utilizing lightweight distributed ledger technology to create tamper-proof, autonomous machine-to-machine micro-payment economies.
\end{enumerate}
