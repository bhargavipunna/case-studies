\section{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Related to Retail Sales Analysis and Demand Forecasting}
Engineering implementations must align with broader global sustainability imperatives. By modernizing legacy operations, eliminating resource inefficiencies, and ensuring transparent institutional governance, this case study directly supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\subsection{SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth}
\noindent\textbf{Target 8.2:} Achieve higher levels of economic productivity through diversification, technological upgrading and innovation. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 8.5:} Achieve full and productive employment and decent work for all women and men.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Optimizes enterprise supply chains and demand forecasting, reducing inventory write-offs and operational overhead.
  \item Eliminates repetitive manual computation, transitioning human personnel toward high-value analytical supervision.
  \item Empowers businesses with robust decision intelligence to thrive in complex multi-channel markets.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} optimizes supply chain inventory and commercial forecasting, driving operational productivity and mitigating commercial waste.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production}
\noindent\textbf{Target 12.2:} Achieve the sustainable management and efficient use of natural resources. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 12.5:} Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Curtails agricultural water and fertilizer wastage through closed-loop soil moisture and salinity telemetry.
  \item Prevents retail stock obsolescence and perishable spoilage via high-precision inventory demand forecasting.
  \item Improves industrial asset lifespans through predictive wear-and-tear monitoring, curbing premature hardware disposal.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} prevents retail stock over-allocation and food spoilage through precision demand forecasting and dynamic safety stock optimization.
\end{itemize}
