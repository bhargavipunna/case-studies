\section{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Related to Deep Learning-Based Medical Image Classification for Early Disease Detection}
Engineering implementations must align with broader global sustainability imperatives. By modernizing legacy operations, eliminating resource inefficiencies, and ensuring transparent institutional governance, this case study directly supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\subsection{SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being}
\noindent\textbf{Target 3.8:} Achieve universal health coverage, including financial risk protection, access to quality essential health-care services and safe, effective, quality pharmaceuticals. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 3.d:} Strengthen the capacity of all countries, in particular developing countries, for early warning, risk reduction and management of national and global health risks.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Facilitates rapid disease risk prediction and diagnostic triage via non-invasive biomedical sensors and machine learning.
  \item Enables continuous real-time physiological telemetry, alerting clinical specialists instantly during acute anomalies.
  \item Democratizes preventive care and early diagnostic screening for underserved and remote patient populations.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} facilitates rapid, automated medical image triage and early disease detection, reducing diagnostic errors and saving lives.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure}
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.1:} Develop quality, reliable, sustainable and resilient infrastructure to support economic development and human well-being. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.4:} Upgrade infrastructure and retrofit industries to make them sustainable, with increased resource-use efficiency. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 9.5:} Enhance scientific research, upgrade the technological capabilities of industrial sectors, and encourage innovation.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Deploys resilient edge sensors, distributed telemetry backbones, and robust network routing architectures.
  \item Modernizes industrial plant monitoring with predictive maintenance, preventing catastrophic equipment downtime.
  \item Fosters high-performance technological innovation through reproducible, benchmarked algorithmic implementations.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} advances industrial deep learning innovation through scalable computer vision and intelligent automated infrastructure.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities}
\noindent\textbf{Target 10.2:} Empower and promote the social, economic and political inclusion of all, irrespective of age, sex, disability, race, ethnicity, origin, religion or economic or other status. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 10.3:} Ensure equal opportunity and reduce inequalities of outcome.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Removes geographical barriers to expert medical triage through automated remote patient diagnostics and telemetry.
  \item Provides equitable educational support frameworks that identify disadvantaged or struggling students objectively.
  \item Promotes inclusive technology access through low-cost, open-source embedded and distributed computational architectures.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} democratizes specialist-grade clinical diagnostic imaging and smart infrastructure access across resource-constrained regions.
\end{itemize}
