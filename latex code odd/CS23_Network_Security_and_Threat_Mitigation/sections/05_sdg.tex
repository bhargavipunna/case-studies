\section{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Related to Network Security and Threat Mitigation}
Engineering implementations must align with broader global sustainability imperatives. By modernizing legacy operations, eliminating resource inefficiencies, and ensuring transparent institutional governance, this case study directly supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\subsection{SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions}
\noindent\textbf{Target 16.5:} Substantially reduce corruption and bribery in all their forms. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 16.6:} Develop effective, accountable and transparent institutions at all levels. \\
\noindent\textbf{Target 16.10:} Ensure public access to information and protect fundamental freedoms, in accordance with national legislation.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Secures critical cyber infrastructure, enterprise endpoints, and communication channels against illicit intrusions.
  \item Guarantees data integrity, confidentiality, and non-repudiation across public and private computational networks.
  \item Enforces algorithmic transparency, verifiable model evaluation, and auditable security access rules.
  \item \textbf{Case Context:} safeguards public and institutional data networks against malicious cyberattacks, ensuring institutional transparency and cybersecurity.
\end{itemize}
